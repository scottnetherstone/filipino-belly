Our Story

From Our Kitchen
To Your Table

The Filipino Belly brings the bold, vibrant flavors of the Philippines straight from a small village in Misamis Oriental, Cagayan de Oro to your table here in Wimauma, Florida.

Every dish is crafted from recipes passed down by her father Moises and brothers — the same Moises whose silhouette graces our logo. From crispy, golden lumpia to savory adobo simmered to perfection, each bite carries the flavors of home.

We offer daily orders and drop-off catering for parties and events of all sizes. Pre-orders are always welcome!

Mercy Williams — Head Chef & Co-Owner
